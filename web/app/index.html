What this is, and what it is not

  • Educational, not diagnostic. Not medical advice. Talk to a clinician or genetic counselor about real concerns.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Your file is read with your browser's FileReader and analyzed on this page. Open your Network tab: no request carries your file.
  • Ancestry is a coarse estimate. Continental scale only, not calibrated to you, and can be wrong for mixed or under-represented ancestry.
  • Polygenic scores are relative positions, not risks. Not percentiles or diagnoses, and not adjusted for your ancestry (see your ancestry section).
  • Limited by your array. Consumer tests read only a fraction of the genome. Every section shows how many markers were found.
  • Cookieless analytics. Anonymous page views via Cloudflare, never anything about your DNA.

Free and open source. Ancestry from 1000 Genomes Phase 3. Polygenic scores from the PGS Catalog (Lambert et al., Nature Genetics 2021 and 2024). Trait and variant alleles verified against NCBI dbSNP and ClinVar.
