What this is, and what it is not

  • Educational demonstration, not a medical or diagnostic test. Nothing here is medical advice. Do not make health decisions from it. Talk to a clinician or genetic counselor about real concerns.
  • One published research score. It computes a single polygenic score from the PGS Catalog, shown with full attribution below once your file loads.
  • Not calibrated to you. A raw polygenic score is not a percentile, a probability, or a risk. It is not adjusted for your ancestry, which strongly affects how these scores should be read.
  • Limited by your array. Consumer tests genotype only a fraction of a research score's variants, so coverage is partial. The result shows exactly how many variants were found.
  • Cookieless analytics. This page counts anonymous page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookies, no fingerprinting). It records that the page was viewed, never anything about your DNA.
