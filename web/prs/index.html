What this is, and what it is not

Built to be understood, not oversold.

  • Educational, not a medical test. Not medical advice. Talk to a clinician or genetic counselor about real concerns.
  • One published score. A single polygenic score from the PGS Catalog, fully attributed below once your file loads.
  • Not calibrated to you. A raw score is not a percentile, probability, or risk, and is not adjusted for ancestry, which strongly affects how it reads.
  • Limited by your array. Consumer tests cover only a fraction of a score's variants. Your result shows exactly how many were found.
  • Cookieless analytics. Anonymous page views via Cloudflare, no cookies, no fingerprinting. Never anything about your DNA.
