About DNAnalyzer

Your DNA is yours. We read it without taking it.

A free, open source project that turns your 23andMe or AncestryDNA file into insights, without sending your genome anywhere.

Analyze my DNA Read the code
No account. Free. Runs in your browser.
Our mission

Learn from your genome without giving it away.

Your genes are the most personal data you own, and you cannot change them after a leak.

So we read the file you already have, in your browser, with code anyone can inspect. No server holds your data.

How we stay honest

Built to be checked, not believed.

Every claim here you can verify yourself.

Private by design

Never uploaded. Watch your Network tab.

Open source

Real, cited data

PGS Catalog, 1000 Genomes, ClinVar, dbSNP.

Honest about limits
