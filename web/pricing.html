Questions

The honest answers.

Is my data really private?

Yes. Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Check the Network tab yourself.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every core feature is free forever, with no account and no paywall.

How do you make money?

We do not sell data. Optional supporters fund a Hack Club sponsored nonprofit.
